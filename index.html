A shared approach

What is disciplinary literacy?

Every subject has its own ways of making sense of information and communicating ideas. Disciplinary literacy helps students read, write, interpret representations, and use evidence in ways that fit the subject they are learning.

These atlases help teachers recognize that work inside the curriculum and bring it into focus during lesson planning.
